Stock Demand Prediction

Upload retail transactions, watch each analysis stage, then explore measured weekly sales predictions.

Analyze a dataset

Use an Excel workbook (.xlsx, one or more sheets) or CSV with these columns: Invoice, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, Price, Country. The UCI Online Retail II workbook is a ready-to-use example.

Connecting to the analysis server…

Requires at least 32 complete weeks and a product with 20 sales weeks before the final 12-week test. Maximum file size: 64 MB. Processing runs on this computer; the raw upload is deleted after analysis.
